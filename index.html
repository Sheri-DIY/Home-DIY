Meet Sheri

I used to feel
intimidated too.

Hi, I'm Sheri — a working mom of two girls, a Respiratory Therapist, and a passionate home DIYer based in South Surrey, BC. Since 2017 I've been updating our fixer-uppers, first our townhouse and now our detached home.

I learned alongside my husband and dad, and for a long time I felt completely out of my depth. The intimidation was real. Not knowing which brush to use, which caulk to buy, whether I was doing it right. Sound familiar?

After years of learning, making mistakes, and figuring it out room by room — I'm passionate about making sure other women don't have to feel that way. You don't need experience. You don't need a contractor. You just need someone to show you how.
